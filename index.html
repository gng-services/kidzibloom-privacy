Kidzibloom Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Kidzibloom is a collection of games for children, run by GnG Service Private Limited ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information. Contact: thegngservices@gmail.com.

The short version

  • No ads. No analytics. No tracking. No accounts for children.
  • We do not collect, sell or share personal information about children.
  • Everything the app remembers stays on your device.

What is stored on the device

  • A parent PIN, stored only as a salted, encrypted hash. It is never sent anywhere.
  • Settings: chosen age groups, sound, play limits.
  • Game progress (stars, levels reached, which games were played). This holds no name, photo, location or contact details.

This data never leaves the device. It is excluded from Android backup and device-to-device transfer. Uninstalling the app, or using Grown-ups > Reset, deletes it.

What we do not do

  • We do not show advertising or use the advertising ID.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or attribution services that identify users.
  • We do not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.
  • We do not offer in-app purchases or links that leave the app for a child.

Children

Kidzibloom is designed for children aged 3 to 12 and is meant to be set up by a parent or guardian. Because we collect no personal information from children, there is nothing to request, correct or delete on our side. For questions about this policy or to ask us about data, write to thegngservices@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
